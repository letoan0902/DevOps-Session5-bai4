==========================================================================
 SO DO GITFLOW - BAI 4 (Hotfix & Gitflow thuc te)
 Chi dung ky tu ASCII. Thoi gian di tu TRAI sang PHAI.
 Ky hieu: o = commit, + = diem re nhanh / diem gop, [..] = tag.
==========================================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PHAN 1. Gitflow tong quat: feature va release
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

 main       o [v1.0.0] ---------------------------------- o [v1.1.0] --------->
            |                                              ^
            |                        release/1.1.0         | (merge --no-ff + tag)
            |                      +---o---o---o-----------+
            |                     /                         \
 develop    o---o---o-------o----+---o-------o---------------+---o---o--------->
                 \         /      \         /
 feature/*        +---o---+        +---o---+
                  (feature A)      (feature B)

 - feature/*: tach tu develop, gop lai vao develop.
 - release/*: tach tu develop, gop vao main (co tag) VA gop nguoc vao develop.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PHAN 2. Gitflow tong quat: hotfix
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

 main       o [v1.1.0] --------------------------+ [v1.1.1] ----------------->
             \                                  /
 hotfix/*     +----o---------o-----------------+
                                                \
 develop    ---o-------------o---------o---------+--------------------------->

 - hotfix/*: tach TRUC TIEP tu main (ban dang chay production).
 - Gop vao main + gan tag tang PATCH, sau do gop ca vao develop
   (hoac vao release/* neu dang ton tai mot nhanh release).

   508c835 Merge branch 'hotfix/v1.0.1' into develop


 main            bb7ec50 --- 78569ed ---------------------------------- 337d9b5
                  (init)    [v1.0.0]\                                  /  [v1.0.1]
                           |         \                                /
 hotfix/v1.0.1             |          +--- 01b4647 --- 2101781 ----+
 (xoa sau khi xong)        |                fix:         chore:     \
                           |                                         \
 develop                   +--- 1297c15 --- 20a228e -----------------+--- 508c835
                               feat:        feat:                      merge hotfix
                               report       roadmap                    vao develop


 Cach doc:
   1. 78569ed la diem chung: main (tag v1.0.0) va develop cung tach ra tu day.
   2. hotfix/v1.0.1 tach TRUC TIEP tu 78569ed tren main, khong mang theo
      1297c15 va 20a228e (tinh nang dang lam do cua develop).
   3. 337d9b5 la merge commit (2 cha: 78569ed va 2101781) tren main, gan tag v1.0.1.
   4. 508c835 la merge commit (2 cha: 20a228e va 2101781) tren develop,
      dua ban va 01b4647 + 2101781 vao develop ma khong dong vao tinh nang dang lam.
